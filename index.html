Advocate Profile

Advocate Abid Shaikh is a highly qualified and dedicated legal professional holding degrees in LL.B., and an LL.M. With a profound understanding of Indian jurisprudence and a commitment to justice, Adv. Shaikh brings rigorous academic training and practical courtroom acumen to every brief he handles.

He actively practices before the Constitutional Courts and Trial Courts, ensuring that clients receive comprehensive legal strategies from the trial stage up to appellate litigation.

Primary Courts of Practice:

Family & Matrimonial
